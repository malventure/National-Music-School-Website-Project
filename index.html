About Us

Welcome to The National Music School! We are dedicated to providing high quality music education to students of all ages and skill levels.

We believe that music education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or experience.

Our experienced instructors are passionate about music and are committed to helping students achieve their musical goals.

Why Choose Us?

Our instructors are not just teachers; they are working musicians who tailor each lesson to your individual goals. We focus on building a solid technical foundation while nurturing your creativity, ensuring that every student learns to play the music they love.

Ready to start your musical journey? Book your first lesson today!

Upcoming Events

Join Us for a Joyous Celebration of Music!

Every year on June 21st, the world comes together to celebrate World Music Day—a global event that honors the beauty and power of music in all its forms. This spring, The National Music School is thrilled to invite you to our World Music Day Spring Showcase, a heartwarming evening that promises to be our most exciting event yet.

Picture this: a stage filled with students proudly performing the pieces they've worked so hard on all year. A piano student plays their first classical sonata. A young guitarist strums an original rock riff. A voice student belts out a jazz standard. And a string ensemble fills the room with a soaring melody from another corner of the world. That's the magic of our Spring Showcase—a true celebration of growth, creativity, and the universal language of music.

This year's event is extra special as we honor the diversity of musical traditions from around the globe. Our performers will present pieces spanning classical, folk, rock, jazz, and world music, reflecting the rich variety of genres and instruments taught at our studio. Whether you're a parent cheering on your child, a fellow musician, or simply a music lover, you'll find something to inspire you.

The World Music Day Spring Showcase is free and open to the entire community. Bring your family and friends—the more, the merrier! Let's celebrate the joy of music together.





But the showcase is more than just a performance—it's an opportunity. Exceptional performers will be recognized for their dedication and artistry, with top students receiving special awards, certificates, and the chance to be featured in our annual gala. For our younger musicians, like 6-year-old Maya, this is a wonderful first taste of stage success.

It's a chance to build confidence, receive professional feedback, and take a meaningful step forward in their musical journey.
